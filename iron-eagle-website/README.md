## Before You Launch — Fill These In

Open `index.html` and update:
- `ADD YOUR PHONE NUMBER` → your actual number
- `ADD YOUR EMAIL` → your business email
- Your exact street address if you want it listed

## Free Hosting (Netlify — Easiest)

1. Go to [netlify.com](https://netlify.com) and create a free account
2. Click **"Add new site"** → **"Deploy manually"**
3. Drag and drop this entire `iron-eagle-website` folder
4. It'll give you a live URL instantly (something like `iron-eagle.netlify.app`)
5. Once you buy your domain, go to Site Settings → Domain Management → add custom domain

## Domain + Business Email

1. Buy domain at [Namecheap.com](https://namecheap.com) (~$12/year)
   - Recommended: `ironeagletruckcenter.com`
2. Point it to Netlify (they walk you through it)
3. For business email (`you@ironeagletruckcenter.com`):
   - **Free option:** [Zoho Mail](https://zoho.com/mail) — free for 1 user
   - **Google option:** Google Workspace — $6/month, works with Gmail
